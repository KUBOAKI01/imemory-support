关于这个 App / About

「记忆」是一个本地优先的个人记忆记录工具。支持文字、照片、视频、声音混排记录，自动生成标题，一次点击记录位置，回收站保护误删内容。

iMemory is a local-first personal memory journal: mixed text, photo, video and audio entries with automatic titles, one-tap location capture, and a trash to protect against accidental deletion.

常见问题 / FAQ

数据存在哪里？/ Where is my data stored?

文字与索引保存在你的设备上；开启同步后，所有内容通过你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库在登录同一 Apple ID 的设备间同步。开发者无法读取你的任何数据。

Text and indexes are stored on your device. With sync enabled, everything syncs across your devices through your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot read your data.

如何开启同步？/ How do I enable sync?

在 iPhone 系统设置中登录 Apple ID 并开启 iCloud，App 会自动开始同步，无需注册任何账号。

Sign in to iCloud in iOS Settings; syncing starts automatically. No sign-up is required inside the app.

删除的数据可以恢复吗？/ Can I recover deleted data?

普通删除会进入回收站，保留期（7/30/60/90 天或永不，可在设置中调整）内可随时恢复。回收站中永久删除的数据不可恢复。

Deleted items go to Trash and can be restored during the retention period (7/30/60/90 days or never, adjustable in Settings). Items permanently deleted from Trash cannot be recovered.

隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-10-06

中文

「记忆」（iMemory）不收集、不上传、不分析你的任何个人数据。

  • 你的全部记忆内容（文字、位置、照片、视频、声音）仅保存在你的设备和你本人的 iCloud 私有数据库中；
  • iCloud 同步由 Apple 的 CloudKit 服务提供，数据归属于你的 Apple ID，开发者无法访问；
  • 本应用不包含第三方 SDK、广告、统计或追踪组件；
  • 应用权限（相机、麦克风、定位）仅在你主动使用对应功能时调用，定位仅在你主动记录时获取一次；
  • 卸载应用不会删除你 iCloud 私有库中的数据；如需彻底删除，请同时删除应用并前往 iCloud 设置管理存储。

English

iMemory does not collect, transmit or analyze any personal data.

  • All your content (text, location, photos, videos, audio) stays on your device and in your own iCloud private database;
  • iCloud sync is provided by Apple's CloudKit service. Data belongs to your Apple ID and is inaccessible to the developer;
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs, ads, analytics or tracking;
  • Camera, microphone and location permissions are used only when you actively use the corresponding feature. Location is captured once, only on request;
  • Uninstalling the app does not remove data from your iCloud private database. To delete permanently, remove the app and manage storage in iCloud settings.
